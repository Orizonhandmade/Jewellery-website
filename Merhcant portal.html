1. Daily Maintenance

Remove fine jewellery before swimming, exercising, or applying lotions and perfumes to preserve metal sheen and gemstone clarity.

2. Gentle Cleaning

Soak gold and diamond pieces in warm water with mild dish soap for 10 minutes. Gently brush with a soft-bristled toothbrush.
